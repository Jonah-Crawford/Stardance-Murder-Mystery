# Stardance Murder Mystery

A ~30-45 minute murder mystery visual novel featuring members of Stardance.

Craw has apparently been murdered during the release of Crawssembly 2.0.

Unfortunately, almost everyone in the room appears to have helped.

Investigate the scene, question the Stardancers, reconstruct the murder, and work out what actually happened at 20:07.

Free. Extremely serious. Definitely not slop.


Made with renpy, also available for download on [itch.io](https://thecraw.itch.io/stardance-murder-mystery)
