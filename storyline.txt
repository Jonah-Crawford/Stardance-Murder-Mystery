                                        Premise

Craw is being investigated heavily by an exaggerated caricature of Fraud Squad, while Crawssembly 2.0 is about to release.

He decides to fake his death just after releasing the update, wait for the investigation to blow over, and reappear quietly.

He tells nobody of the plan, instead he distributes tiny jobs among several people, each of whom thinks they're helping with the launch, rather than the faked death.

Someone gets asked to write a strange `.craw` program
Someone else helps with lighting
Someone else provides hardware
Someone else schedules something
Someone tests networking

Nobody possesses enough info to understand what they're building, and thus, after the apparent murder, everyone has something they're reluctant to admit they helped Craw with.

                                        Timeline

17:30 - Setup begins

Stardance members arrive at the launch venue
People set up laptops, separate project demonstrations, decorations, food, etc...
Craw is unusually busy and keeps disappearing, which gives lots of characters small and innocuous observations:

"I saw craw carrying a cable downstairs"
"He asked me where the maintenance room was"
"He borrowed my power bank"
"He wanted to know whether the Wi-Fi reached the basement"

18:15 - Craw prepares the escape system

Craw secretly finishes preparing the fake-death mechanism, underneath the stage is an small corridor which leads to an old server room in the basement with electronically controlled doors, where the fatal engineering mistake lies.

The escape route is:

Stage --> backstage --> service corridor --> security door --> server room --> second exit --> outside

During the fake death, Craw escapes through the stage mechanism and traverses this route, a Crawssembly program controls part of it, because of course it does.

18:47 - First suspicious interaction

Someone catches Craw working on something unrelated to the launch on his laptop, and he immediately closes it. This can become a witness statement later on.

19:00 - The party begins

The opening point of the game, with the light-hearted introduction sequence. The player walks around, and exchanges basic small-talk with some people, to introduce the cast, whilst encountering several minor clues before the player even knows they're clues.

19:09 - Craw receives an FS message

A visible event, Craw gets a message and his expression changes, but he denies any issue.

19:30 - Project showcase

Other stardance members present their projects, which gives alibis to some characters, perhaps provide a programme of events as evidence.

19:50 - Craw secretly arms the system

Craw is seen leaving the audience to go to his laptop. This is to arm the fake-death mechanism. 

19:55 - Craw's final normal conversation

The player has a seemingly normal conversation with Craw, where after he leaves to start his presentation

20:00 - Crawssembly 2.0 presentation begins

Lights dim, Craw gets on stage, and makes a grand entrance. It starts with a sincere presentation, but at the end he announces one final demo.

20:07 - The "Murder"

Lights suddenly go out, and a crash is heard, the emergency lights come back on and Craw is down, blood is visible. Nobody saw what happened during the ~5 seconds of darkness.

Someone runs on stage, and panic engulfs the crowd. The electrical failure/smoke effect forces everyone away for a moment outside the building, but once the player returns, the body is gone.

20:08 - Craw enters the service corridor

The fake death worked perfectly, once Craw reaches the door to the server room, the, still running, laptop remotely opens the door, but the second door doesn't open to let him out of the room, there is a bug in the Crawssembly code. With no way to leave the old server room, Craw is trapped. This is the exact moment the plan fails.

20:10 - The investigation begins

From everyone else's perspective, Craw has just been murdered. From Craw's perspective, he's locked himself in a basement.

The player should now start collecting evidence from witnesses and the core characters

20:12 - The schedules messages begin

Craw is dead, but he appears online in the #stardance channel, the global "group chat" that all characters use.

A message appears, automated from Craw's laptop, from which mania ensues from the crowd. Theories become:

Craw schedules it
Craw isn't dead
The account has been hacked
A bot sent the message

This is the first major hint that some obvious interpretation is incorrect.

20:17 - A Git commit appears

Another impossible event, on the Crawssembly repo a new commit pushed by Craw appears. Current theories get galvanized.

??:?? - Craw cutscene

Cut to black, with an unknown person (Craw) being trapped in a black room

20:30 - Evidence suggests foul play

The laptop is recovered showing the script used to cut the lights. The player finds that the core characters helped in the creation of the code, hardware, and setup. Because Craw intentionally distributed his preparations, everyone included looks guilty. This creates conspiracy within the wider crowd.

The player now does the classic "Who dunnit?" series of events to pin the blame on one of the core characters

21:00 - Act I Climax

The player has assembled a classic murder theory, and accuses one of the core characters. But a sudden new development causes that theory to be impossible. The theory collapses and it's concluded that it was a fake death.

Act II and Act III are not time dependant.

Act III:

The player properly examines the method of escape, and the bug in the code is found. The entire mystery ultimately hinges upon Craw making a programming error.

The player opens the door to the server room, and Craw is inside.

                                      Clues

Every major revelation should take no fewer than 3 clues. The player should be able to suspect each answer before the game confirms it.

Proposition A: nobody murdered Craw.

The player should eventually be able to prove that from multiple angles: the blood wasn't real; the apparent attack was initiated automatically; nobody could have removed the body in the available window; perhaps nobody actually saw a wound; the blackout/smoke sequence was prepared before the event.

Proposition B: Craw deliberately staged his death because of FS.

Again, multiple clues: the FS message at 19:09; strange preparatory requests; the automation code; evidence of a planned escape route; scheduled post-death activity; perhaps something showing he'd prepared to be absent afterwards.

Proposition C: Craw intended to escape but became trapped.

This is the cleverest deduction and should therefore be the hardest. Evidence that he expected to be elsewhere after 20:30; something scheduled assuming he'd escaped; no evidence that he actually reached the outside; finally, the bug in the door-control code.

Impossible Fact: The body couldn't have been removed.

CCTV footage by the doors, where the crowd evacuated after the smoke, shows all main characters leaving with no body, showing that none of the suspects could have carried off the body.

Body moved -> Craw is alive -> No murder
